# Request for Quotation (RFQ) Template
**Document ID:** RFQ-CC-V3-2026-001  
**Subject:** Sourcing of Photonic Camera Module Components for Cosmic Camera v3.0.0  

---

## 1. Company Overview
[Your Company Name] is developing the Cosmic Camera v3.0.0, a high-performance photonic camera module for flagship mobile devices. We are seeking partners for high-volume component supply and assembly.

## 2. Technical Specifications & Requirements
Please provide quotes addressing the following technical points:

1.  **Sensor Interface:** Support for MIPI CSI-2 (D-PHY v2.0 or C-PHY v1.2) with at least 4 lanes.
2.  **Resolution & Pixel Size:** Primary sensor must achieve 50MP with a minimum pixel size of 1.0µm (native) or 2.0µm (binned).
3.  **Optical Design:** Lens stack must be 7P or higher, with an aperture of f/1.8 or wider and a Total Track Length (TTL) < 6.5mm.
4.  **Sensing Wavelength:** VCSEL flood illuminator must operate at 940nm (±10nm) with a Field of Illumination (FOI) matching the camera FOV (min 80°).
5.  **ISP Capabilities:** Dedicated hardware acceleration for 4K/60fps HDR video, real-time depth mapping, and low-light noise reduction.
6.  **Safety Compliance:** All active emitters must meet IEC 60825-1 Class 1 Laser Safety standards under all operating conditions.
7.  **Power Consumption:** Active mode power consumption must not exceed 600mW at full 50MP/30fps throughput.
8.  **Reliability:** Components must pass 2000 hours of Temperature/Humidity Bias (THB) testing and 1000 cycles of Thermal Shock.
9.  **Form Factor:** Maximum module dimensions must not exceed 12mm x 12mm x 6.8mm (including housing and connectors).

## 3. Commercial Requirements
- **Target Volume:** 100,000 units per month (initial), scaling to 1,000,000 units.
- **Price Quotation:** Unit price at 100k, 500k, and 1M tiers.
- **Lead Time:** Maximum 8 weeks from PO to delivery.
- **Samples:** Provide 20 Golden Samples (GS) for initial validation within 2 weeks.
