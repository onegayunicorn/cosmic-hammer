# Validated Suppliers: Cosmic Camera v3.0.0

The following suppliers have been identified as primary partners for the Cosmic Camera v3.0.0 hardware procurement, based on their capacity for high-volume mobile module production and technical compliance.

## Tier 1: Core Imaging Components
1. **Sony Semiconductor Solutions Corp.**
   - **Role:** Primary CMOS Sensor Supplier (IMX series).
   - **Strength:** Market leader in mobile imaging sensors; highest SNR and low-light performance.
   - **Contact:** [sony-semicon.com](https://www.sony-semicon.com/)

2. **Samsung Electronics (System LSI)**
   - **Role:** Secondary Sensor / High-Res Specialist (ISOCELL).
   - **Strength:** High resolution (108MP+) and competitive pricing for secondary modules.
   - **Contact:** [samsung.com/semiconductor](https://www.samsung.com/semiconductor/)

3. **Largan Precision Co., Ltd.**
   - **Role:** Precision Lens Assembly.
   - **Strength:** World leader in aspherical plastic lenses (up to 8P); exclusive patents for thin-profile mobile lenses.
   - **Contact:** [largan.com.tw](http://www.largan.com.tw/)

4. **Sunny Optical Technology (Group) Co., Ltd.**
   - **Role:** Lens Stack & Module Integration.
   - **Strength:** Strong vertical integration from lens components to full camera module assembly (CCM).
   - **Contact:** [sunnyoptical.com](http://www.sunnyoptical.com/)

## Tier 2: Processing & Sensing
5. **Onsemi**
   - **Role:** ISP (Image Signal Processor) & Interface.
   - **Strength:** Specialized ISPs (AP1302) with advanced HDR and noise reduction for mobile.
   - **Contact:** [onsemi.com](https://www.onsemi.com/)

6. **ams OSRAM**
   - **Role:** Photonic/IR Emitters (VCSEL).
   - **Strength:** Proven track record in 3D sensing and face recognition VCSEL arrays.
   - **Contact:** [ams-osram.com](https://ams-osram.com/)

## Tier 3: Interconnects & Passives
7. **Hirose Electric Co., Ltd.**
   - **Role:** FPC / Board-to-Board Connectors.
   - **Strength:** Ultra-low profile connectors optimized for high-speed MIPI signals.
   - **Contact:** [hirose.com](https://www.hirose.com/)

8. **Murata Manufacturing Co., Ltd.**
   - **Role:** Passive Components (Capacitors/Inductors).
   - **Strength:** Miniature MLCCs (01005 size) critical for compact camera modules.
   - **Contact:** [murata.com](https://www.murata.com/)

---
**Verification Status:** All Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers are validated for ISO 9001 and IATF 16949 compliance.
